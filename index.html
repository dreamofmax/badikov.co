Biography

Maxim Badikov is a London-based photographer, picture editor and photography collector. He works on long-form projects that explore the social landscape. With a background in journalism and experience as a picture editor for leading media, he brings a strong storytelling approach to creating compelling, authentic visuals.

Badikov is available for commercial and editorial commissions.

For project inquiries or to purchase his work, please reach out via email.
