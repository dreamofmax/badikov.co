Biography

Max Badikov is a documentary photographer based in London, available for assignments across the United Kingdom and worldwide. His practice centres on long-form documentary and editorial photography — sustained bodies of work exploring social landscapes, communities and everyday life.

Alongside his own work, Badikov is a picture editor with experience across news media, and a photography collector who has spent years gathering vernacular and archival images from flea markets and private sources around the world.

He shoots across film and digital formats, with a particular interest in black-and-white photography, bringing the same editorial eye — honed selecting images for publication — to his own documentary and portrait work.

Badikov is available for commercial and editorial commissions worldwide. For project inquiries, editorial assignments, or to purchase his work or pieces from his collection, please reach out via email.
